A Dot Product Is
All You Need

Emad Mostaque

A single positive dot product, read under the stated rules, determines the quantum structure generated by a free scalar field: its Hilbert space, evolution, flat relativistic symmetry and Born weights. Two scales remain free: mmm and ℏ\hbarℏ.

1The axiom

Axiom. Displacements are measured by a single positive dot product ggg.

Fix a real affine space of dimension D≥2D\ge 2D≥2. The dot product on its translation space determines lengths, orthogonality, reflections and a Laplacian Δg\Delta_gΔg​. A reading is a number Φ(f)=∫fφ\Phi(f)=\int f\varphiΦ(f)=∫fφ: a real scalar field φ\varphiφ sampled by a real Schwartz test function fff. The axiom fixes the geometry, not the readings. A statement about them carries a probability, and the joint distribution of every finite list of readings is the law. Which law does the axiom determine?

What the axiom leaves open is governed by probability: its sum and product rules, symmetry, and maximum entropy. By the axiomatic method nothing else is assumed. For readings these say:

Sum rule
Laws are nonnegative, normalized and countably additive on exclusive (orthogonal) events; a sublist has the marginal law.
Product rule
Independently prepared parts multiply; selecting an event makes it certain and keeps the ratios of its subevents.
Equal values
Equivalent descriptions receive equal values: those related by isometries of ggg, of the covariance form and of the state space.
Maximum entropy
Given the lattice site and edge mean squares that ggg measures (§2.3), the law maximizes entropy, assuming nothing else.

Theorem 1 (Determination). Under the stated rules, with the lattice moments specified in §2.3, for each pair of scales m,ℏ>0m,\hbar>0m,ℏ>0 the axiom determines exactly one law of the readings: the centred Gaussian law with covariance ℏ(−Δg+m2)−1\hbar(-\Delta_g+m^2)^{-1}ℏ(−Δg​+m2)−1. Its readings, paired with their reflections in a plane, generate, uniquely up to a unitary preserving them, a state space with unitary dynamics, tensor composition, Born probabilities and ideal projection conditioning. The resulting theory satisfies the von Neumann and Wightman axioms.

4The axioms

Under the stated rules, the resulting free scalar theory satisfies the von Neumann axioms for states and measurement and the Wightman axioms for the field.
